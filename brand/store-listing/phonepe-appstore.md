## Title

CyDrip: Ride & Run Tracker

## Short description (≤200 characters)

Free, open-source ride and run tracker. Big live speed, a 3D street map with turn-by-turn routes, GPS that records with the screen off, and share images. No account, no ads.

## Long description (≤4,000 characters)

CyDrip turns the phone on your handlebar into an instrument cluster. It is free, open source and has no account, no ads and no analytics. Everything you record stays on your phone.

RIDE
• Big live speed you can read at a glance, with distance, ride time, average and max speed, climb and calories
• A tilted 3D street map that follows you, built from OpenStreetMap
• Simple view: a grid road view that works without mobile data
• Portrait or landscape, for any handlebar mount
• The screen stays on while you ride, and finishing takes a press-and-hold so a bump can't end your ride

WHERE TO?
• Search for a place, street or landmark and see the route on the map before you start
• Cycling routes for rides, walking routes that can use footpaths for runs
• The next turn, how far away it is and the distance left, always on screen
• Take another way and CyDrip finds a new route from where you are

RUN
• Pick Run and the big number becomes your pace per kilometre
• Splits for every kilometre
• The screen sleeps so your phone can stay in your pocket while GPS keeps recording

RECORDING YOU CAN TRUST
• Keeps recording with the screen off or while you use another app
• Auto-pause at traffic lights and stops
• Smoothed GPS for accurate distance and clean routes

SNAP
After every ride or run, make a share image of your route and your numbers. Four styles, including a transparent sticker for your own photos, in story or square format. Share it or save it to your gallery.

YOUR WEEK
• Every ride and run with its map, speed and elevation charts
• This week against last week, an 8-week chart, totals, personal bests and your streak

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no sign-up
• No ads, no analytics, no tracking
• Rides are stored only on your device
• Place search and routes come from free OpenStreetMap services, only when you use Where to?
• Location is used only while you record or plan a route

OPEN SOURCE
CyDrip is MIT licensed and built in the open by Webrizen AI Labs:
https://github.com/webrizen-ai-labs-pvt-ltd/cydrip

CyDrip is designed for general fitness and wellness only. It is not a medical device and is not intended for medical use. Speed, distance, climb and calorie figures are estimates. Always watch the road, not your phone.
