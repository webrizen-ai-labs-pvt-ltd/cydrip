Your handlebar, as an instrument cluster.

CyDrip is a free, open-source ride and run tracker for Android. Big live numbers, a 3D map of the streets around you, and GPS that keeps recording with the screen off.

Download for Android View the source

Free APK, no account. Version 1.1.0, MIT licence.

What's on the dash

Glanceable speed
Speed you can read at 30 km/h, with distance, time, average, max, climb and calories underneath. Portrait or landscape mounts.
3D street map
A tilted map that follows you, with real streets and buildings from OpenStreetMap. Out of data? Switch to the grid road view.
Ride or run
Runs show pace per kilometre and split every km. The screen sleeps so your phone can stay in your pocket.
Screen off, still recording
Background GPS with auto-pause at lights. Finishing is press-and-hold, so a pothole can't end your ride.
Snap
After every ride, an image of your route and your numbers. Four styles, including a transparent sticker for your own photos.
Your week
This week against last, eight weeks at a glance, personal bests and your streak.

Your rides never leave your phone.

  • No account. Open the app and ride.
  • No analytics. Nothing is collected about you.
  • Open source. Read every line on GitHub, MIT licensed.

Install

  1. 1

    Download the APK

    Get the latest version from GitHub Releases on your Android phone.

  2. 2

    Open it

    Android asks you to allow installs from your browser the first time. Allow it, then tap Install.

  3. 3

    Allow location while using the app

    That's all CyDrip needs. If your phone closes apps in the background, set CyDrip's battery use to Unrestricted.
